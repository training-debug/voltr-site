01 The programs

Pick the one that fits this block. Run the others after.

01

Athletic Strength

For people who want to be strong and move like it. Power work opens every session, then one heavy lift, then the accessories and carries that make the strength usable.

Split
3 days: lower + power, upper + rotation, hinge + carry
Session
60 to 75 minutes
Progression
Load on main lifts weekly, deload in week 4, top-set test in week 8
Key lifts
Back squat, bench press, trap bar deadlift, jumps, med-ball throws, carries
02

Hybrid

Strength and running in the same week without one ruining the other. Heavy legs are placed at least 48 hours before the quality run, and lifting stays at a level that keeps the runs good.

Split
3 strength sessions + 3 runs: easy, quality, long
Running
Written in minutes, zones and effort, not pace
Progression
Run time up about 10% a week, deload week 4, 3 km time trial in week 8
Key lifts
Back squat, RDL, bench press, chin-ups, split squats, Nordic curls
03

3-Day Minimum

For blocks with travel and full weeks. Three full-body sessions of 45 minutes. Every exercise has a hotel-gym or dumbbell version, so a trip changes the equipment, not the plan.

Split
3 full-body sessions, paired sets, fixed rest
Session
45 minutes
Progression
Double progression, pivot week 4, more sets and effort from week 5
Travel
Hotel / dumbbell alternative for every exercise

04 Questions

Before you buy.

Who are these programs for?

People with at least a year of consistent gym training who know the main lifts. If you're just starting out, these are not the right first step.

What equipment do I need?

A regular gym: barbell, rack, bench, dumbbells, cables, a trap bar if available. 3-Day Minimum also lists a hotel-gym or dumbbell version for every exercise.

How do I get the programs?

Right after payment you land on a page where you download all three files. You also get the receipt by email. Keep the files, they're yours.

Is this the VOLTR app?

No. The app is retired. The programs replace it: one payment instead of a subscription, and nothing to log in to.

Can I upgrade to coaching later?

Yes. Book a free intro call any time. If we work together, the program you ran gives us a useful starting point.

Can I get a refund?

Because the files are delivered immediately, the right of withdrawal ends once you have access. If something is wrong with your files, write to training@voltr.at and I'll sort it out.

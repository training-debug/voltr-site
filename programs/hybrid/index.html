03 Fit

Who it's for.

Built for
+Adults with at least a year of consistent lifting who can run 30 minutes without stopping and want strength and running to improve together.
+Equipment: Full gym: barbell, rack, trap bar, dumbbells, cable station, landmine. A watch with a timer. A heart rate strap helps.
Not built for

04 Questions

Before you buy.

How do I get the program?

Right after payment you land on your download page. Save the PDF to your phone and the spreadsheet to your laptop or Google Drive. Lost the link? Write to training@voltr.at and you get it again.

What format is it?

Two files. A PDF that reads well on your phone, and a spreadsheet for logging your loads in Google Sheets, Excel or Numbers.

Can I move days around?

Yes. The rules for moving sessions, missed sessions and bad weeks are part of the program.

Is this the VOLTR app?

No. The app has been retired. The programs replace it: one payment, nothing to log in to, yours to keep.

Refunds?

Because the files are delivered immediately, the right of withdrawal ends once you have access. If something is wrong with your files, write to training@voltr.at and I will sort it out.

Can I switch to coaching later?

Any time. Book a free intro call. The weeks you logged give us a useful starting point.

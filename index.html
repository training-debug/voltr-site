02 How coaching works

You train. I decide what the week has to deliver.

Start

A goal with a date

We agree on one measurable target, how we measure it and when. In writing.

Weekly

I see what actually happened

Not what was planned. What you trained, how it felt, what got in the way.

Weekly

I decide the next week

Load, volume, which run stays, which session moves. Built around your calendar, not an ideal one.

Weekly

You get the reasoning

A few lines on why the week looks the way it does. You see the decision, not just the plan.

Always

A direct line to me

Questions go to me, not to a team. Replies within 24 to 48 hours.

That decision is the coaching.

06 About

At 22 I trained every single day. That wasn't the answer.

I wanted to be strong and fit at the same time, so I kept adding more. What actually moved me forward was the opposite: less junk volume, better execution, and people around me who came from competitive sport.

I still train like that today. Heavy lifting, long runs, and a calendar that rarely looks the same two weeks in a row.

The people I coach now are rarely 22. They have careers, families and travel. They still try to train like they're 22. My job isn't to give them more to do. It's to decide what deserves their time.

08 Questions

Answered briefly.

What happens on the intro call?

Thirty minutes, video or phone. We look at where you are, what you're working towards and what has been getting in the way. If coaching makes sense, I'll explain how it would look for you. If it doesn't, I'll tell you that too.

I travel a lot. Does this still work?

That's what it's built for. Hotel gyms, time zones, three days instead of five: the week gets planned around what's actually ahead, not the other way round.

Why a three-month minimum?

Because anything shorter doesn't allow an honest answer. Twelve weeks is enough to see whether the work is landing. That's also why there is no trial month.

Is the VOLTR app still available?

No. The app is retired. If you want structure without coaching, the three VOLTR Programs replace it: one payment, all three, yours to keep.

Do you only coach in Vienna?

No. Coaching runs online, wherever you are. In Vienna, in-person sessions are possible on request.

What equipment do I need?

A gym you can train in regularly. Everything else is built around what you actually have access to, including hotel gyms on the road.

Do you do nutrition plans?

No meal plans. If you eat somewhere different every day, a fixed plan is useless. We work with decisions you can make in the situation you're actually in.

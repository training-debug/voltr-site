07 Questions

Answered briefly.

What happens on the intro call?

Thirty minutes, video or phone. We look at where you are, what you're working towards and what has been in the way. If coaching makes sense, I'll show you how it would look for you. If it doesn't, I'll say so.

What happens after I sign up?

You complete a short onboarding, we run the assessment and set your goal with a date. Your first program arrives in the app, and the weekly rhythm starts: you train and check in, I review and adjust.

I travel a lot. Does this still work?

It's built for exactly that. Hotel gyms, time zones, three days instead of five: you tell me what's ahead, and the program follows your trip, not the other way round.

Why a three-month minimum?

Anything shorter doesn't give an honest answer. Twelve weeks is enough to see whether the work is landing, which is also why there's no trial month.

Do you only coach in Vienna?

No. Coaching runs online, wherever you are. In Vienna I also train people 1:1 in person, in a five-star hotel gym in the city centre or where you already train, by arrangement. Full Accountability combines both: remote coaching and in-person sessions, wherever you are in the world.

What equipment do I need?

A gym you can train in regularly. Everything else is built around what you have access to, hotel gyms included.

Do you write nutrition plans?

No meal plans. If you eat somewhere different every day, a fixed plan is useless. We work with choices you can make in the situation you're actually in.

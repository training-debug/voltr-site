Privacy

This page explains which personal data is processed when you use voltr.at. Controller: Lukas Plassnig e.U., Wehrbrücklstraße 6/7, 1220 Vienna, Austria, training@voltr.at.

Hosting

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub Inc., USA). When you visit the site, your browser transmits technical data such as your IP address, date and time of the request and browser type. This is necessary to deliver the website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). GitHub may process this data in the USA; transfers rely on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and standard contractual clauses.

Fonts

All fonts are hosted on this server. No connection to Google Fonts or other font services is made.

Booking an intro call (Calendly)

The booking calendar is provided by Calendly LLC, USA. When you load the calendar or book a call, Calendly processes the data you enter (name, email, your answers) and technical data. Legal basis: steps prior to entering into a contract at your request (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Calendly's privacy policy: calendly.com/privacy.

Payments (Stripe)

Payments for coaching and programs are processed by Stripe Payments Europe Ltd., Ireland. Stripe processes your payment data to execute the payment (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Stripe's privacy policy: stripe.com/privacy.

Email and messages

If you write to us by email, we process your message to answer it (Art. 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR). If you subscribe to the newsletter, it is sent via Kit (ConvertKit LLC, USA) based on your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you can withdraw at any time with the unsubscribe link.

Coaching

If you become a coaching client, we process the data needed to deliver the coaching, including training data and, with your explicit consent, health-related information you share with us (Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR). Training is delivered via Everfit.

Cookies and tracking

This website itself sets no tracking cookies. Embedded services (Calendly, Stripe Checkout) may set cookies that are necessary for their function.

Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection. Contact training@voltr.at. You can also lodge a complaint with the Austrian Data Protection Authority (dsb.gv.at).

03 Questions

Before you buy.

Who are these programs for?

People with at least a year of consistent gym training who know the main lifts. If you are just starting out, these are not the right first step.

How do I get the program?

Right after payment you land on your access page, and the same link arrives by email. Open the PDF on your phone and tap once to copy the log sheet into your own Google account. Lost the link? Write to training@voltr.at and you get it again.

What format is it?

A PDF that reads well on your phone, and a Google Sheet for logging your loads. An Excel version is included if you prefer not to use Google.

Can I move days around?

Yes. The rules for moving sessions, missed sessions and bad weeks are part of the program.

Is this the VOLTR app?

No. The app has been retired. The programs replace it: one payment, no app, no subscription, yours to keep.

Refunds?

Because the files are delivered immediately, the right of withdrawal ends once you have access. If something is wrong with your files, write to training@voltr.at and I will sort it out.

Can I switch to coaching later?

Any time. Book a free intro call. The weeks you logged give us a useful starting point.
